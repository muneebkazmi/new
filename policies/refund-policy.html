Returns & Replacements

We sell across Pakistan on Cash on Delivery. You pay the courier when the parcel reaches you, so you are never out of pocket before you have the item in your hand.

Before it ships

We confirm every order by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch. If you want to cancel or change anything — the address, the colour, the item — tell us at that call and it costs you nothing. You can also message us any time on WhatsApp +92 305 3488833 between 9am and 9pm.

Damaged, faulty, incomplete or not what you ordered

Contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will replace the item or refund you in full. This covers anything that arrives broken, does not power on, is missing parts from the box, or is simply not the product you ordered.

To make a claim, message us on WhatsApp or email support@wiredandwarped.com with:

  1. Your name and order number
  2. A short description of the problem
  3. Clear photos of the item and the parcel packaging

Keep the packaging until the claim is settled — for courier damage we usually need to see it.

Changed your mind

Because orders are paid in cash at the door, you can simply decline the parcel if you change your mind before accepting it. Once you have accepted and paid, we cannot take an item back purely because you no longer want it, unless it is faulty or not as described. Electrical grooming items and personal care products cannot be returned once used, for hygiene reasons.

Refunds

Because payment is taken in cash by the courier, approved refunds are paid back to you directly by bank transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash — whichever suits you. We will arrange this with you when the claim is approved, normally within 3 to 5 working days.
