Delivery

We deliver across Pakistan on Cash on Delivery. You pay the courier at your door — nothing in advance, and no card details needed.

How long it takes

  • Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar: typically 2 to 3 working days
  • Other cities and smaller towns: typically 3 to 5 working days
  • Remote areas: may take a little longer; we will tell you at the confirmation call if we expect a delay

Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed late in the evening are processed the next working day.

We confirm before we ship

Every order gets a call or WhatsApp message before it is dispatched. This is how we make sure the address is right and that you actually want the item — it saves you a wasted delivery and saves us a returned parcel.

If we cannot reach you after a couple of attempts, we will hold the order rather than send it blind. Please make sure the phone number you enter at checkout is one you actually answer.

Delivery charges

Delivery is charged at checkout and shown before you confirm. The amount the courier collects at your door is the total shown on your order — item price plus delivery. There is nothing extra to pay.

Tracking

Once your parcel is collected by the courier we will send you the tracking number by WhatsApp or SMS. Allow up to 24 hours after dispatch for the first scan to appear.

If your parcel does not arrive

If tracking says delivered but you have not received it, or the parcel has stalled for more than two working days, message us on WhatsApp +92 305 3488833 with your order number and we will chase the courier for you.

Refusing a delivery

You can decline the parcel at the door if you have changed your mind. We would rather you did that than accept something you do not want. Repeated refusals may mean we ask for advance payment on future orders.
